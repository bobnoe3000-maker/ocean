# First Region — the Rudd Valley

The buildable proof: the western spoke of the bowl, from Thrum under Rudd's Head down the Rudd to Wickery and the western Shelf. Everything in this file is an instance of a system specified elsewhere: the world in [WORLD.md](../world/WORLD.md), the simulation in [LIVING_WORLD](../systems/LIVING_WORLD.md), the quests in [ADVENTURE_ENGINE](../systems/ADVENTURE_ENGINE.md), the fights in [COMBAT](../systems/COMBAT.md), the screen in [SESSION_UX](../systems/SESSION_UX.md), the growth in [PROGRESSION_ECONOMY](../systems/PROGRESSION_ECONOMY.md) and the standing in [CLASSES_AND_STANDING](../systems/CLASSES_AND_STANDING.md).

## 1. The map in words

Stand on the Ring Road at Wickery and look west, up the valley. Behind you, four miles of Shelf to the water. Ahead, the Rudd comes down out of the Wend in a straight green line, and the spoke road follows its south bank. The valley is thirty miles long and never more than six wide, and every place in it is on the river or the road.

**From the top down:**

- **Rudd's Head** — the pass. Snow until Mid Thaw. The road over it goes nowhere the game reaches this region.
- **Thrum** — the Skerrow hold cut into the cliff below the pass: a gate, a hall, galleries running back into the Rim, a forge that never goes out. Hold-mother Brakka; four hundred Skerrow; iron. Two days' walk from Wickery. On the Shoulder above it, an hour's climb by a marked path, **the Rudd Stones**: nine stones on the moor over the river's source, kept by Sarane and Ondrin. A stone-warden sleeps at its centre.
- **The Rudd Wend** — eight miles of forest where the road narrows to a track along the river. Half a day through. Ulder ground off the track. Near its lower edge, on the north bank, **the Hewers' Camp**: Thrum's timber crew, a slide down to the river, and in this Thaw a dam. On the south bank, above Gorse End's pasture, the strip of Wend that ends at **the old ash**, a stone-mark.
- **Fenn's Mill** — where the Wend ends: Bram Fenwright's mill on a race off the Rudd, two miles above the village. The valley's flour.
- **Sallowford** — the home village. Thirty-one souls. A ford (and Osk's ferry-boat for when the Rudd runs high), the Ford Inn, Garrow Tull's smithy, Hild Marrow's house with the drying-loft, Idony Sallow's reeve's table, twelve houses, a green, a well the Ulder bless at Firstwater, a notice post. Above it on the south slope, **Gorse End**: Reyne Gorse's big farm and the pasture where Tobbin keeps the flock.
- **Hobb's Cross** — four miles down, where the spoke road meets a farm lane: Wat Hobb's inn and cart-yard, a barn, a stable that hires a mule. The crossroads is where the Wrack watch the road.
- **The Rudd Bridge** — four miles on: the stone bridge where the spoke road meets the Ring Road, and the Rudd, now wide and slow, crosses under. Every cart from the valley crosses it. The Stair wants a toll-house on its Wickery end.
- **Wickery** — the town, four hundred souls, on the Present Shore where the Rudd meets the Shelf: a wall (earth and timber, defence 30), the reeve's house, the Moot's watch-house, the Stair's counting-house with its yard and the Chain's barracks, Marrock of Thrum's iron-yard, the Post inn on the quay, the notice board at the market cross, the quay itself with the **low-mark post** (a notched timber that shows the Still's fall, year by year), and the boats drawn up on the mud below.
- **The western Shelf** — beyond the quay, four miles of cracked mud and reed to the water. On it: **the Salt Pans** (the Stair's boiling-sheds, where Shelf water is evaporated for salt), **the Old Shore road** (the drowned-age track along the first-age shoreline; sheep are grazed on it), **Corrow's Reach** (the Wrack's camp: turf huts round a fire in a hollow, a palisade of driftwood, defence 10), and **the Sunk Court** (§6), a ring of stone halls half out of the silt, surfaced in 605 C.

Sites in the region: 14 named above plus 26 minor (farmsteads, fords, Wend clearings, Shelf pans and reed-beds): 40 in the engine's tables. Travel times by road at 3 miles per hour: Sallowford–Fenn's Mill 1 h; Sallowford–Hobb's Cross 1 h 20; Hobb's Cross–Rudd Bridge 1 h 20; Rudd Bridge–Wickery 1 h; Sallowford–Wickery 3 h. Sallowford–Hewers' Camp through the Wend 2 h (4 h in early Thaw); Hewers' Camp–Rudd Stones 3 h (6 h). Wickery–Sunk Court across the Shelf 2 h (4 h).

## 2. Why this valley is the first region

It is a cross-section of the one page: Rim, Shoulders, Wend, Lowmark, Shelf and water in a day's walk; all four peoples living where they live (Skerrow at Thrum, Ulder at the stones and in the Wend, Vael in the village and town, Kest in the counting-house); four powers present and the fifth (the Stair's seat) reachable by letter; the Age of Stone in the Court, the Cutting in the Skerrow contract, the Age of Ships in the ledger. And it is the valley the hero was born in, so the first Hand deeds are for neighbours whose names the player already knows.

## 4. Three powers present, and their opening moves

Five powers act on the valley; three have a face and a seat in it, and two act from its ends.

**The Moot, through Aud Penhallow at Wickery.** Resources in the valley: one militia band (quality 1) at Wickery's wall; Dell's watch (quality 1; 2 after a Drill); Sallowford's band (quality 1; Osk's); sway 70 at Wickery, 75 at Sallowford; no coin (the Moot's chest at Wickery holds 30 marks, all of it owed). Goals here: no toll-house at the Rudd Bridge; the Wrack off the Ring Road; the levy filled. **Opening moves:** Thaw 3 *Levy* at Wickery (six from each village, thirty days); Thaw 7 *Post* ("The Moot will pay for word of the Wrack's camps"); Thaw 27 *Address* to the hero at Name if regard ≥ +20; Thaw 32 *Send band* to the Rudd Bridge if the Stair builds (a clash the Moot loses unless the hero is in it).

**The Stair, through Vesk Idren at the counting-house.** Resources: coin 600 in the Wickery chest; the Chain (one band, quality 2, Drusk); the debt ledger (Fenn's Mill 3 marks Quarter-day and 8 arrears; Sallowford 3; eleven farms; Wickery's Moot chest 30); the Salt Pans; a contract with Thrum for salvage labour. Goals here: every valley debt called or paid by Reckoning; salvage rights to the Court; the toll-house. **Opening moves:** Thaw 1 Quarter-day collected (Sabel rides the valley); Thaw 3 *Call debt* on Fenn's Mill (late); Thaw 12 *Send band* to the Salt Pans (after Ilune's working); Thaw 20 *Salvage* at the Court (a Skerrow crew, paid); Thaw 24 *Seize* Fenn's Mill if unpaid; Thaw 30 *Build* the toll-house (five days, 60 marks, two Chain bands — the second raised Thaw 25); Thaw 70 *Call debt* on three farms after the Quarter-day interest doubles.

**The Wrack, through Halse Corrow at Corrow's Reach.** Resources: two bands at the Reach (quality 2; one is Pell's), the Court's outer halls, coin 120, and every debtor the Stair calls. Goals here: Wickery's counting-house burned; the Rudd Bridge watched; the Court held against the Stair's crews. **Opening moves:** Thaw 1 riders along the Old Shore road at dawn (the Firstwater anchor's sighting: a show, not a raid); Thaw 3 *Send band* (Pell's) to the Rudd Bridge; Thaw 7 *Raid* Hobb's Cross (foragers take Wat Hobb's stores; Moot sway at Hobb's Cross −15); Thaw 20 *Address* the hero at Name (Corrow's letter); Thaw 45 and Thaw 75 *Raise band* from recruits; Thaw 82 *Raid* Wickery with three bands (the season's end, if the season is not played).

**From the valley's ends:** the **Holds** (Brakka, through Marrock): Thaw 3–5 *Build* the timber-slide dam at the Hewers' Camp; Thaw 16 *Parley* with Reyne Gorse for the ash strip; Thaw 26 *Fell* whatever the contract allows; Thaw 60 *Send* masons to the bridge for the Stair's stone. The **Quiet** (Sarane and Ondrin): Thaw 3 *Ward* — hold the Rudd for ten days against the timber-slide (which keeps Ondrin from the Firstwater blessing, which is the first thing the village notices); Thaw 16 Ondrin at the ash; Thaw 40 *Address* the hero at Name if Quiet regard ≥ +10.

## 6. The dungeon — the Sunk Court

**Reason.** A court of the Age of Stone on the western Shelf: nine halls in a ring round a well, built when the bowl's floor was dry, drowned in the Drowning, and surfacing since 605 C. as the Still falls. The Wrack use its outer court as a second camp. The Stair pays Skerrow to cut its stone-bronze. The Holds want the bronze for themselves. The Quiet want it left, because the well-ring — the bronze band round the central well — is what holds the third layer sealed, and what is in the third layer is what the Court's Drawers became. Kit Ashby's brother drowned in the first layer in 607, when it was still wet.

**Layers.** Each is a state four powers have goals about.

**What runs out.** Oil (two flasks for a layer-1 run, four for the Salt Hall, and the salt halves them); water (none until the cistern; a Mage who enters with a full skin has 100 for the Steps and the stair); bandages (a layer-2 run without a Draw companion uses four to six); and world time — a full descent to the Salt Hall and back is a world day, during which the powers move.

**Cost to failure.** A death in the Court leaves the pack at the fall point ([COMBAT](../systems/COMBAT.md) §8); Wrack sentries strip it within a day unless Wrack regard ≥ +30; a companion left below is dead unless fetched within two days, and the layer's state may have changed (a hall that floods is a hall that drowns a body). The rumour "went into the Court and came out without them" is a weight-5 memory with everyone on the quay and a permanent line in Kit's greeting. A hero who dies in the Well is found by the Wrack and wakes at the Reach owing Corrow.

**Why it matters to play.** Every power's goal in the valley has a line to this place: the Stair's salvage, the Holds' bronze, the Quiet's seal, the Wrack's camp, the Moot's fear of what walks out. Vo2 flooding the Salt Hall changes it permanently into a boat-and-breath dungeon (the fisher trade's hour). Nothing in it is required for the campaign's road: a player can leave the Court to the powers, and the season table shows what that costs.

## 7. The first twenty hours

Traced for a **Fighter, trade smith**, playing twenty hours across seven real days at the launch (six evenings and a few short check-ins), with the offline rule ([LIVING_WORLD](../systems/LIVING_WORLD.md) §1) advancing the calendar between sessions.
