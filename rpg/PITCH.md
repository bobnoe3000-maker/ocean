# Hearthfall — Pitch

## The world, in three sentences

The [Hearth](world/WORLD.md) is a bowl: a ring of mountains, the Rim, notched once at the south by the Fall, the only way in or out; inside, four rings of land (Shoulders, Wend, Lowmark, salt Shelf) round a falling lake, the Still. Four peoples share it: the **Ulder**, who keep the standing stones and teach Drawing; the **Vael**, who farm the Lowmark and are most of everybody; the **Skerrow**, who hold the Rim and cut the Fall; the **Kest**, who came up it ninety-one years ago and own the ledgers; behind them three ages: Stone drowned, the Cutting drained, Ships wrote it down. Five powers want incompatible things (**Moot**: untolled roads; **Stair**: every debt in its book; **Holds**: timber and bronze; **Quiet**: the Shelf left alone, or the Fall closed; **Wrack**: no lord, no ledger), and magic is **Drawing**, which takes water from whatever is nearest, including you, and leaves salt that never washes out.

## From nobody to a force

A villager of Sallowford with a trade (smith, herbalist, fisher, carter), a class (Fighter, Mage, Rogue) and standing 0. [Standing](systems/CLASSES_AND_STANDING.md) is **renown**: who holds a memory of you, weighted by their reach. At Hand the notables ask you first; at Name a factor offers you the Chain and an outlaw writes; at Voice two powers write in one morning; at Force the seats court or oppose you.

## The loop of a session

Three minutes: the away page, [one map jump](systems/SESSION_UX.md), one errand — Pell's letter into Idony's hand, the glyph ember to flame. Fifteen: a whole quest — break the Skerrow dam, get the river loosed at the Rudd Stones, and Fenn's Mill grinds. An evening: the Sunk Court, or the clash at the Rudd Bridge. Each ends with a change the player can name; [autobattle](systems/COMBAT.md) plays the same hero and wins what a thumb wins.

## The loop of a campaign

The [powers move at dawn](systems/LIVING_WORLD.md) whether you play or not: a dam, a called debt, a toll-house, a burned counting-house. One [valley](slice/FIRST_REGION.md) is one season of ninety days and one conflict you can tilt; five regions are the bowl, and the last question is whether the Fall stays open.

## Where adventures come from

No quest list. The [engine](systems/ADVENTURE_ENGINE.md) reads a **trigger** in the simulation — a stopped wheel, a band on a bridge — finds the person with the reason, and fills seven slots: giver, reason, stake, place, opposition, twist, consequence. Names come from the peoples' tables, the place is the site whose state caused it, stakes scale with standing from a lost flock to a border war, and outcomes write back.

## What is sold

Cosmetics; side-chapters, spare valleys; conveniences the world already sells (a mule at Hobb's Cross, Merrin Hale's almanac). Never experience, coin, gear, companions, standing, revives or time skips. No gacha, no energy, no daily anything. [Every store line](systems/PROGRESSION_ECONOMY.md) names the in-world way to the same thing, or it is a colour.

## Three things this is not

1. **Daggerfall generated from templates; this generates from state.** Bram's wheel stopped because the Holds dammed the Rudd and the Quiet held the river; the quest names him and sits on the dam.
2. **Wizardry's party is rolled; this one is met.** Corva wants out by the Stair, Ilune answers to the stones, either can leave, and one left in a dungeon is dead for good.
3. **Tolkien's weight is a chosen one and deep time; here there is neither.** No prophecy, no named gods: a falling lake, and magic priced in your own body's water.
