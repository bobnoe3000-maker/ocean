# Hearthfall — Pitch

## The world, in three sentences

The [Hearth](world/WORLD.md) is a bowl: a ring of mountains, the Rim, notched once at the south by the Fall — the only way water leaves, and the only way in for anyone who cannot mine; inside, four rings (Shoulders, Wend, Lowmark, salt Shelf) round the Still, a lake falling a finger a season since the Skerrow cut that notch to save a mine and the river began wearing it deeper, so each year the mud gives up a court. Four peoples share it, each blaming the fall on what suits them: the **Ulder**, who keep the stones and teach Drawing; the **Vael**, who farm the mud and are most of everybody; the **Skerrow**, who hold the Rim and are right; the **Kest**, who came up it ninety-one years ago and own the ledgers — behind them three ages: Stone drowned, the Cutting drained, Ships wrote it down. Five powers want incompatible things (**Moot**: untolled roads; **Stair**: every debt in its book; **Holds**: the sill armoured, timber and bronze for it; **Quiet**: the Shelf left alone, or the Fall closed; **Wrack**: no lord, no ledger), and magic is **Drawing**, which takes water from whatever is nearest, including you, and leaves salt that never washes out.

## From nobody to a force

A villager of Sallowford with a trade, a class (Fighter, Mage, Rogue) and standing 0. [Standing](systems/CLASSES_AND_STANDING.md) is **renown**: who holds a memory of you, weighted by their reach. At Hand the notables ask first; at Name a factor offers the Chain; at Voice powers write; at Force the seats court or oppose you.

## The loop of a session

Three minutes: [a map jump](systems/SESSION_UX.md), Pell's letter into Idony's hand, the glyph ember to flame. Fifteen: break the Skerrow dam, loose the river at the Rudd Stones, Fenn's Mill grinds. An evening: the Sunk Court, or the clash at the Rudd Bridge. Each ends with a change you can name; [autobattle](systems/COMBAT.md) wins what a thumb wins.

## The loop of a campaign

The [powers move at dawn](systems/LIVING_WORLD.md) whether you play or not: a dam, a called debt, a toll-house, a burned ledger. One [valley](slice/FIRST_REGION.md) is one season and one conflict you can tilt; five are the bowl, and the last question is the sill — armour it, leave it cutting, or close the Fall.

## Where adventures come from

No quest list. The [engine](systems/ADVENTURE_ENGINE.md) reads a **trigger** — a stopped wheel, a band on a bridge — finds the person with the reason, and fills seven slots: giver, reason, stake, place, opposition, twist, consequence. Names come from the peoples' tables, the place is the site whose state caused it, stakes scale with standing, outcomes write back.

## What is sold

Cosmetics; side-chapters; conveniences the world already sells (a mule at Hobb's Cross, Merrin Hale's almanac). Never experience, coin, gear, companions, standing or revives. No gacha, no energy, no dailies. [Every store line](systems/PROGRESSION_ECONOMY.md) names the in-world way to it, or is a colour.

## Three things this is not

1. **Daggerfall generates from templates; this generates from state.** Bram's wheel stopped because the Holds dammed the Rudd; the quest names him and sits on the dam.
2. **Wizardry's party is rolled; this one is met.** Corva wants out by the Stair, Ilune answers to the stones, either can leave, one left below is dead.
3. **Tolkien's weight needs a chosen one and deep time; here there is neither.** No prophecy — a falling lake whose cause you can walk to, and magic priced in your own water.
