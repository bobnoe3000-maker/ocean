# Ocean — Look Bible (phase 1 look test)

This document stands alone. It describes the target look in words, names the
five reference frames the critic scores against, fixes the hero vista, and
lists the materials. Copyrighted reference frames, when present, live in
`refs/` (gitignored) and never ship.

## 1. Reference frames

### R1 — Sea of Conquest: harbour approach, golden hour
A three-master enters a walled colonial harbour from the lower left, seen from
a gull-height tilted top-down camera. **Light**: sun low and to the right, deep
orange-gold; shadows from masts and buildings stretch left across water and
quay at four to five times object height; shaded walls pick up blue from the
sky, shaded sand picks up warmth bounced from lit walls. **Water**: deep water
a saturated blue-violet, a broad glitter path of orange sparkles under the sun,
turquoise-green over the sand bar at the harbour mouth, foam only where swell
meets the mole and at the ship's bow. **Materials**: lime-washed walls with
ochre and dusty pink variants, terracotta roofs with lichen and missing tiles,
stone quay dark and wet at the waterline, tarred hull with a pale wale stripe,
canvas warm cream. **Palette**: `#F2A15A` sun-side walls, `#5B6FA6` sky-lit
shade, `#1E3A7A` deep water, `#3FA394` shallows, `#F7D9A6` sail highlights,
`#8C3B22` roofs.

### R2 — Sea of Conquest: port at night
The same kind of port under a high moon. **Light**: everything cool blue-grey
from the moon except warm pools around lanterns on posts along the quay and
amber windows; the sky is a dark navy with visible stars and a thin milky band;
moonlight silver on wave backs, no grey ambient floor, true darkness in the
alleys. **Water**: near black, a narrow moon-glitter path, lantern reflections
as warm vertical streaks that break up with the chop. **Materials** read only
where lit: wet stone glints, plaster is a cool grey, the hull is a silhouette
with rigging lines catching the moon. **Palette**: `#0B1430` sky, `#111C3C`
water, `#FFB259` lantern warm, `#8FA3D6` moonlit surfaces, `#F6C97A` windows.

### R3 — Dredge: Greater Marrow at dusk
A small harbour town on a rock, seen from a boat at dusk with fog rolling in.
**Light**: sun gone, sky a graded slate-blue to pale apricot at the horizon;
lighthouse and windows are the only warm sources and each one has a soft halo
in the humid air. **Water**: dark green-grey, low contrast, gentle swell,
foam only against the pilings. **Materials**: weathered grey timber, rusted
iron, mossy stone, everything desaturated but still textured. **Palette**:
`#3B4A5C` sky, `#2C3B3A` water, `#C9B79C` timber, `#E7A85C` windows.

### R4 — Dredge: open water in fog
Boat and wake in dense fog, rocks as layered silhouettes. **Light**: flat,
nearly shadowless, a bright disc where the sun is. **Atmosphere**: three
distinct depth layers, each rock a lower-contrast, bluer-greyer version of the
last; nothing far reads as sharp. **Water**: grey-green, only the wake and the
near chop carry detail. **Palette**: `#B8C2C6` fog, `#6F8A8C` near water,
`#8FA0A4` far silhouettes.

### R5 — Photograph: Mediterranean island harbour at golden hour
Described in words (a typical view of Symi or Hydra from the hill above the
harbour, sun about ten degrees above the sea). **Light**: sun-facing walls
orange-cream, shadows long and blue-violet, shadow edges soft at fifty metres
and crisp at five. **Water**: green-gold over the sandy shallows beside the
quay, then an abrupt line to deep cobalt; boat reflections broken into
horizontal strokes by the chop. **Materials**: lime wash stained by rain
runoff below windows, ochre wash flaking at the base of walls, terracotta tile
rows uneven with darker replacements, stone quay edge rounded and wet, rope
coils and fenders on the quay. **Palette**: `#F4B77C` lit walls, `#6B6FA8`
shadowed walls, `#1F3F8A` deep water, `#6FB29A` shallows, `#2E5A3F` scrub.

## 2. The hero vista

- Scene: one island with a walled harbour on its south-east side, a lighthouse
  on the mole, a stone quay with a timber dock, eleven buildings climbing the
  slope, palms along the beach to the west, a cliff to the north.
- Time: 17:30, sun elevation 12°, azimuth 277° (west-north-west). The camera
  looks along azimuth 142°, so the sun sits 135° behind-right of the view
  axis: every camera-facing wall, the sails and the back slope are front-lit
  and warm, long shadows fall up-left across the water (as in R1 and R5). The
  glitter path belongs to the noon frame, where the sun is high enough to
  sparkle straight down into the camera.
- Camera: pitch 52° from horizontal (the brief's 58° reads as a map from 240 m
  up; 52° keeps the tilted top-down read while façades and the slope show),
  yaw fixed, 120 m of visible water width in portrait, target on the harbour
  basin. The quay and town fill the upper
  third, the anchored brig sits on the lower-left third line, the lighthouse
  on the mole tip on the lower-right third line, the harbour mouth between
  them at the bottom of the frame.
- Ship: the brig anchored just inside the harbour mouth, bow into the wind,
  topsails loosed to dry, pennant streaming to leeward.
- Wind: 6 m/s from the port quarter of the ship (south-south-west), so the
  swell runs toward the north-east and the palms lean north-east.
- What the eye does: enters on the brig's sunlit sails, follows the brig's
  long shadow and the turquoise shallows up to the quay, rests on the warm lit
  walls and the lighthouse.

**Status in this environment**: `polyhaven.com`, `ambientcg.com` and
`sketchfab.com` are denied by the egress policy of this session (HTTP 403 on
CONNECT, recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md`). The prompt allows procedural
sources, so every set above is substituted by a procedural PBR set generated
by `tools/gen-textures.mjs` (albedo, normal, roughness, ambient occlusion,
height) at 1024², encoded to KTX2. Each set is built to carry the same wear
story as the intended photographic set: rain streaks under windows, tar
pooling between planks, lichen on tiles, wet darkening at the waterline. When
the photographic sets become reachable they drop in by name with no code
change; the substitution is logged per set in `assets/CREDITS.md`.
